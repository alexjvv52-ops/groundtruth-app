# 4. The morning loop ranks work by path to cash
**Law.** The morning loop ranks work by path to cash — not by what looks
busy.
**Why.** A solo grower has one scarce resource: attention before the day
starts. Software that shows everything shows nothing. Today exists to put
the single highest-leverage action first — the thing that turns a promise
into a delivery and a delivery into money — and to push the rest below it.
**What it forbids.**
- Busywork theater at the top of the queue. Tidy-up tasks, cosmetic
  reminders, and "you have 37 things" counters never outrank collecting
  what is owed or covering what is short.
- A morning that starts on a dashboard. The app opens on Today, the action
  queue. Physical state lives on Farm; money lives on Money.
- Reordering by recency or by what the software finds interesting.
**What it still allows — the signed order.**
1. **Collect** — money owed on delivered orders.
2. **Unmatched** — money that arrived and could not be applied to a row.
3. **Shorts** — promised trays the shelf cannot cover; sow-by dates that
   are already unreachable are labelled as such rather than hidden.
4. **Standing demand → sow** — the weekly promise and the sow that covers it.
5. **Cover, harvest, captures** — confirm what actually happened, by weight.
6. **Deliver** — harvested trays are the evidence for "deliver", not the date.
Receipts and settled work sort below live work. Health appears on Today
only when it is Unhealthy; Degraded stays on the Health screen.
**How you can tell.** The rank is signed as a decision, and the queue is
computed from the record each time it is drawn. When the promised-vs-sown
gap closes and the money is in, Today goes quiet on its own.
