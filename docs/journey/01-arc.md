# The arc
Groundtruth was built money-first. The order was not an accident and it
was not the order most farm apps choose.
## 1. An identity that cannot be confused, then a record that cannot be forked
The first commits forged the app's identity and its guards: a stamp on
every farm file at creation so a restore can refuse a stranger's database,
a verify-replay that refuses directories it did not write, and an
observation cache in which every value from somewhere else carries its age
and its origin. Health checks were on the shared severity engine from the
first week, rendered from the checks that actually ran so that none could
be stubbed. Three days in, the freeze came off: the app became the sole
writer of farm truth, with the previous system kept only as a fallback.
## 2. The money core
Before any polish, the defining audit: does the record lie anywhere? Nine
residuals came out of it and were closed in two days — money debts reach
Today as collect, deliver, and cover; money attention is a standing
condition rather than a one-shot notification; sow-by dates are computed
from growth days and unreachable gaps say so; Health gains money checks and
can no longer read calm while money is owed; one remaining-capacity formula
that the retail door cannot ignore, with harvest evidence — not a date —
retiring a claim; operator-defined crops; an undo that refuses to record a
lie; receivables and the income trail in the export. Then the doors were
hardened: silent shortfall became an explicit write-off, unpriced lines
were refused at both settlement doors, and a blank price could no longer
enter through the order path.
## 3. Today is an action queue, Farm is physical truth
The app was split so that it opens on the ranked queue and keeps the
physical state on its own screen. The queue's order was signed, not
tuned: collect, unmatched, shorts, standing demand, cover and harvest,
deliver. Long lists collapse to a signed default. Health got two scopes
and a gated recovery path.
## 4. Books: the same money, more angles, never a second ledger
Read-only lenses were added over the existing money — shared cash reader,
cash out, net, write-offs, bad debt, category readers — deliberately with
no way to enter money from them.
## 5. The phone as an instrument
The rack-side path was built in stages: a proposal ledger with Confirm on
the PC, a field terminal that pairs and pushes, a dock that serves the PC's
own snapshot on the local network behind an admin token, and then the
Field Instrument spine — eleven fences landed in a single day — that lets a
phone see the same integrity model with two PC-stamped ages and never write
to it. Two proposals were declined on the record rather than deferred: a
step vocabulary on the wire, and TLS on the LAN dock. `03-field-instrument.md`
has the detail.
## 6. Hygiene under speed
Formatter and lint clean with warnings as errors, an unused-symbol purge,
a pinned toolchain, corrected freeze comments that had claimed tests which
did not exist, and a set of integrity corrections: a failed read renders
Unhealthy, only a succeeded and covering refund reaches the order book, and
one foreign import goes through a single door as a one-shot cutover.
## 7. The rooms a business needs
With the record honest, the missing rooms were added without removing
anything shipped: a till that mints a payment link on a delivered order and
attaches the poll to that row, with a QR on the desk; leftover listings
priced and closable by link or by cash; a render-only invoice with a print
path and an integrity footer; pack sheets, cut sheets, a harvest page and a
run page; standing-week views; last-weighed yield memory; a seed jar with
receipts, on-hand readings, and a Health tail. Each landed as a signed,
bounded change with its own acceptance on the running app.
## 8. The front door
Finally the repository itself: untracking proof receipts that had
accumulated at the root, a rewritten README with the six laws and the
real first-open screen, and this documentation pack.
## What the arc is not
It is not a module count and it is not a finished product. The residuals
in `04-still-open.md` are part of the record, not an apology.
