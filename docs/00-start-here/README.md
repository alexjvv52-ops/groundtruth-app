# Start here — your first morning
This page is for the person at the desk. It is not a feature tour and it is
not an architecture lecture. Read it once, then open the app.
## Before you open it
Groundtruth runs on your Windows machine. There is no account and nothing
leaves the computer unless you export it yourself. Download the installer
marked **Latest** from the Releases page (the root `README.md` has the exact
steps), or build it from source. Windows will warn you about the installer
because it is not code-signed. That is expected.
## The first ten minutes
1. **Open the app.** It lands on **Today**. On an empty farm, Today shows
   "Start here" over five cards: add your first venue, record a standing
   order or drop a sample, sow trays to cover that demand, record your first
   wholesale order, configure the scan endpoint (optional). Under them is one
   line offering to restore a backup or start fresh on this machine.
2. **Add a venue and a standing order.** A venue is a real customer. A
   standing order is what they take per week. Everything else on Today is
   computed from the gap between what you promised and what is on the shelf.
3. **Sow to cover the gap.** The sow sheet pre-fills the shortfall. Eight
   microgreens crops ship ready to use; you can add your own or change their
   growing times. If money left your pocket for seed or supplies, the sow
   sheet and the harvest weight pad both offer "Money just left" — record it
   the day it happens.
4. **Read Today again.** It ranks work by path to cash: collect what is
   owed, cover what is short, confirm what actually happened, record what
   was paid. Do the top items you can do now. Ignore the rest until tomorrow.
5. **Open Money.** What is owed, what was recorded. Unpaid stays unpaid on
   screen. Unpriced stays unpriced. If a number looks wrong, fix the record
   in the app — do not "fix" it in a spreadsheet.
6. **Open Health.** Green is not the goal; true is the goal. If Health says
   something is short, late, or broken, the system is working. Every problem
   is one sentence naming the exact failure and the next action.
7. **Find your farm folder.** At the bottom of Farm, and under Settings ›
   Backup & Export, the app shows where the farm lives on disk. Open it once
   and note the path. That folder is your entire farm. See `../export-and-exit/`.
## The rule for the first week
Keep no parallel "real" spreadsheet. If the app and your memory disagree,
count the shelf and put the count on the record — the shelf wins. If the
app and a spreadsheet disagree, that is a defect to report, not a reason to
trust the soft copy.
## Optional: Books and the phone
- **Books** shows the same money from more angles — cash in, cash out, net,
  write-offs, bad debt. It is read-only. There is no second ledger.
- **Phone.** If the dock is running on the PC, a phone on the same network
  can *see* the same Today, Health, and money picture, and can *capture* a
  proposal at the rack. Proposals become farm truth only when confirmed on
  the PC. The phone does not invent the farm.
## Stop when
You know what to do next to move money, and unpaid, unpriced, and short are
still visible on screen. That is the whole point.
